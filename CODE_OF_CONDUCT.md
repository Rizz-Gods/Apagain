# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Over The Horizon community is intended to be a respectful, constructive space for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Participation should be professional, considerate, and focused on the work.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith while discussing technical disagreements.
- Give actionable feedback about code and ideas, not personal attacks.
- Welcome questions and explain decisions without gatekeeping.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's credentials, private data, or personal information.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them when they occur.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, and persistent disruptive conduct are not acceptable. Publishing private credentials or personal data without consent is also prohibited.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct violates this policy. Reports should be made privately to the project maintainer through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical.

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
